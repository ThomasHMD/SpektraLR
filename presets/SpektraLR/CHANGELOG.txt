What was changed, compared with the spektrafilm profiles
--------------------------------------------------------

v0.1.0 (2026-11-02), spektrafilm 0.3.4 (commit 3bb2c2d), 20 films:

- Each film profile (with its declared print paper for negatives; slide films are
  scanned, not printed) is baked into a 32x32x32 RGB table embedded in a
  Lightroom / Camera Raw creative profile (.xmp), ProPhoto RGB primaries,
  Adobe gamma 1.8 encoding.
- Table input: the Adobe Standard rendering (the profile sets
  crs:CameraProfile="Adobe Standard"). The table undoes Adobe's default ACR3
  tone curve (exact inverse of its "RGBTone" application, DNG SDK 1.7.1), then
  maps the grey that Lightroom renders at L* 50 to an 18 % scene grey.
- spektrafilm runs in LUT mode (spatial and stochastic effects off): no grain,
  no halation, no diffusion in the tables.
- Output is scaled so that the paper white (or the clear slide base) reaches
  L* 100 (luminance only: the paper keeps its tint, the grey stays as neutral
  as spektrafilm prints it), no black point compensation. The
  scene exposure is shifted so that an 18 % grey prints at L* 49.6 (118/255).
- Gamut: spektrafilm defaults of spektrafilm-lut (input gamut compression,
  output compression "cam16ucs"), then clipped to [0, 1].

The presets only reference these profiles and add Lightroom grain
(GrainAmount, GrainSize, GrainFrequency) chosen by film speed.
